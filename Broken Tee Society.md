The [Broken Tee Society](https://www.golfersjournal.com/broken-tee-society/) is the membership community of The Golfer's Journal, and it earns its place here on the strength of the magazine more than the meetups. It began almost as a joke—readers posting photographs of broken tees under a hashtag after the Journal's first issue—and hardened into one of the more thoughtful golf communities going: a quarterly of unhurried, well-made writing and photography, plus a private forum where members find games and host one another. The wager of the thing is that golf rewards attention and storytelling, which is a wager the C&RBGC's notes side makes too.

Mark is a passive member, and the word is exact: this is a community that does its best work on the page, asking little of you but to read it. We count it kin for the wavelength rather than the register—it shares no parliamentary streak, but it shares the conviction that the game is worth writing well about. It sits naturally beside [[Fried Egg Golf Club]] on the editorial side of our ledger.
