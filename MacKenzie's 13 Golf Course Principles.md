Here are Alister MacKenzie's 13 essential features of an ideal golf course, as discussed in the document:

- 1. The course where possible should be [arranged in two loops of nine holes](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000G_00001-007b&startLexposition=0000G&emphasizeText=The+course+where+possible+should+be+arranged+in+two+loops+of+nine+holes).
- 2. There should be a [large proportion of good two shot holes, two or three drive and pitch holes, and at least four one shot holes](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000I_00001-005C&startLexposition=0000I&emphasizeText=There+should+be+this+is+number+two.+There+should+be+a+large+proportion+of+good+two+shot+holes%2C+two+or+three+drive+and+pitch+holes%2C+and+at+least+four+one+shot+holes).
- 3. There should be [little walking between the greens and tees](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000I_00001-005C&startLexposition=0000I&emphasizeText=there+should+be+little+walking+between+the+greens+and+tees), and the course should allow room to [lengthen the course in the future if necessary](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000I_00001-005C&startLexposition=0000I&emphasizeText=the+holes+are+sufficiently+elastic+to+be+lengthened+in+the+future+if+necessary).
- 4. The greens and fairways should be [sufficiently undulating, but there should be no hill climbing](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000L_00001-008O&startLexposition=0000L&emphasizeText=The+greens+and+fairways+should+be+sufficiently+undulating%2C+but+there+should+be+no+hill+climbing).
- 5. [Every hole should have a different character](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000L_00001-008O&startLexposition=0000L&emphasizeText=Every+hole+should+have+a+different+character).
- 6. There should be a [minimum of blindness for the approach shots](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000L_00001-008O&startLexposition=0000L&emphasizeText=there+should+be+a+minimum+of+blindness+for+the+approach+shots).
- 7. The course should have [beautiful surroundings](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000L_00001-008O&startLexposition=0000L&emphasizeText=The+course+should+have+beautiful+surroundings) and artificial features should [blend naturally into the landscape](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000L_00001-008O&startLexposition=0000L&emphasizeText=all+the+artificial+features+should+have+so+natural+in+appearance+that+a+stranger+is+unable+to+distinguish+them+from+nature+itself).
- 8. There should be a sufficient number of [heroic carries from the tee](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000O_00001-0048&startLexposition=0000O&emphasizeText=There+should+be+a+sufficient+number+of+heroic+carries+from+the+tee), alongside an [alternative route open for weaker players](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000O_00001-0048&startLexposition=0000O&emphasizeText=the+weaker+player+with+the+loss+of+a+stroke+or+portion+of+a+stroke+shall+always+have+an+alternative+route+open+to+him).
- 9. There should be [infinite variety in the strokes required](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000O_00001-0048&startLexposition=0000O&emphasizeText=infinite+variety+in+the+strokes+required+to+play+the+various+holes).
- 10. There should be a [complete absence of the annoyance and irritation caused by the necessity of searching for lost balls](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000R_00001-006S&startLexposition=0000R&emphasizeText=There+should+be+a+complete+absence+of+the+annoyance+and+irritation+caused+by+the+necessity+of+searching+for+lost+balls).
- 11. The course should be [so interesting that even the plus man is constantly stimulated to improve his game](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000R_00001-006S&startLexposition=0000R&emphasizeText=The+course+should+be+so+interesting+that+even+the+plus+man%2C+this+is+a+like+a+plus+handicap%2C+a+really+good+player.+The+course+should+be+so+interesting+that+even+the+plus+man+is+constantly+stimulated+to+improve+his+game).
- 12. The course should be arranged so that [long handicap players or absolute beginners can enjoy their round](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000U_00001-008x&startLexposition=0000U&emphasizeText=the+long+handicap+player+or+even+the+absolute+beginner+should+be+able+to+enjoy+his+round) despite high scores.
- 13. The course should be [equally good during winter and summer](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m17ad4w1k5pbc6y4r2y22nm4?endLexposition=0000U_00001-008x&startLexposition=0000U&emphasizeText=The+course+should+be+equally+good+during+winter+and+summer), featuring perfect textures on greens and fairways.
